## Full description (max 4000)
Patchlet is a calm little scrapbook for your everyday memories. Open the app, start today's page, and decorate it with torn paper, washi tape, stamps, flowers, tickets, lace and photos. When you're done, flip through your pages like a real book.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Make one scrapbook page a day. Today's page is always ready for you.
• Over 900 realistic elements: washi tape, torn and patterned paper, newspaper clippings, sticky notes, envelopes, tickets, country stamps, date stamps, clips, flowers, film strips, lace, borders, shapes and doodles.
• Stickers and emoji for a playful touch.
• Add your own photos as polaroids, plain prints or torn-edge prints.
• Write with handwritten, typewriter, serif, script and marker fonts.
• Choose textured paper, plain colour paper, and page borders such as lace, stitching, film and torn edges.
• Design your own cover and end page with your name on them.
• Flip through your whole book with a page-turn animation.
• Save any page as an image or PDF, or print it.
• Build a streak and get a gentle daily reminder.
• Sign in with your email to back up your pages and restore them on a new phone.

Patchlet is made for people who love journaling, scrapbooking and keeping little memories, without the mess of glue and scissors.
